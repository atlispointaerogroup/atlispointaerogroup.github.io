About

One partner across the lifecycle of an aircraft.

AtlisPoint Aero Group is a specialist advisory and aircraft asset-services firm for the aviation businesses and investors who own, operate, and finance aircraft.

We bring advisory, transactions, capital, and hands-on aircraft asset services together under one roof. That means owners, operators, and investors can work with a single partner from a first conversation through a sale, an acquisition, a capital raise, or the physical movement and redelivery of an aircraft. Our flight training practice is led by an active flight school operator with direct experience running collegiate career programs, training fleets, and aircraft sales & acquisitions.

What we do

Our work spans two connected practices. On the advisory and transactions side, we handle sell-side and buy-side M&A, capital raising, business brokerage and remarketing, and the transaction support — title search, escrow, deregistration, and closing logistics — that gets a deal to close. On the aircraft asset-services side, we manage recovery and repossession, ferry and positioning flights, lease transition and redelivery, and ongoing asset and lease management.

Who we serve

We work across the full ecosystem of an aircraft and the businesses around it:

  • FBOs — selling at the best price and terms, acquiring, or raising capital to expand facilities and hangars.
  • Charter / Part 135 operators — growth capital and fleet expansion, or a sale that captures the value of a Part 135 certificate.
  • Flight departments — acquisition, disposition, transitions, and asset oversight for corporate and private operators.
  • Flight schools — capital to add aircraft and instructors, plus partnerships and succession planning.
  • Operators & airlines — fleet movements, transitions, and redelivery across any jurisdiction.
  • Lenders, lessors & investors — recovery, repossession, asset oversight, and remarketing through resale and close.

How we work

Every engagement starts with understanding the aircraft, the business, and the objective. From there we structure the right approach, assemble the parties needed to deliver it, and run the engagement with full operational and regulatory control — through to close, redelivery, or handover, with records in order and the schedule intact.

Wherever you sit — owner, operator, or investor — AtlisPoint is built to be the one partner you call.
