Privacy Policy

How AtlisPoint Aero Group — a specialist advisory and aircraft asset-services firm — collects, uses, and protects information.

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how AtlisPoint Aero Group ("AtlisPoint," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with this website and our advisory, transaction, capital, and aircraft asset-services work. By using this site or contacting us, you agree to the practices described here.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you contact us through our forms, by email, or otherwise, we may collect information you choose to share — such as your name, email address, organization, and the details of your inquiry. Our contact form opens your own email client and does not transmit data to a server on this site.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, basic technical information (such as browser type and pages visited) may be collected automatically by hosting or analytics infrastructure to help keep the site secure and functioning.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies or similar technologies only as needed for core functionality. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

How We Use Your Information

We use information you provide to respond to inquiries, to discuss potential engagements, and to deliver and improve our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Data Retention

We retain information only as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Third-Party Services

We may rely on third-party providers (for example, website hosting or email) that process information on our behalf. These providers are expected to handle information consistent with applicable law.

International Visitors and GDPR

We work with clients and counterparties across jurisdictions. If you are located in a region with data-protection laws such as the GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children's Privacy

This site is intended for business audiences and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
